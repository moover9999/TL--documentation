# Can I get a trial proxy or a free trial before purchasing?

We offer trial access on a case-by-case basis to help new users test our proxy services before committing to a plan.&#x20;

To request a trial, please register on our website at [https://dashboard.torchproxies.com/](https://dashboard.torchproxies.com/) and provide our support team through Discord or Chatbot with the email address you used to register.&#x20;

Additionally, let our support team know which **Residential plan or ISP region** you would like the trial to be activated on. Once submitted, our team will review your request and guide you through the available trial or testing options.
