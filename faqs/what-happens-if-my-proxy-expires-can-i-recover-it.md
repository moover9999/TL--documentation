# What happens if my proxy expires? Can I recover it?

Once a proxy or plan expires, access to the IPs is automatically revoked and they are returned to the available pool. This means the same IPs cannot be guaranteed or recovered after expiration, as they may be reassigned to other users.

To avoid losing access, it’s recommended to **renew your subscription before the expiration date**. If your plan has already expired, you’ll need to purchase a new one to continue usage, but the same IPs may not be available.

{% hint style="info" %}
✅ **Tip:** We recommend renewing 24-48 hours before the expiry date to avoid losing the IPs.
{% endhint %}
