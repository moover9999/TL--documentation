# Why are my proxies not working or failing to connect?

There are several common reasons why proxies may not connect or function as expected:

1. **Authentication Issues** – Ensure your IP or username/password is correctly whitelisted and matches the authentication method you selected.
2. **Expired or Inactive Plan** – If your subscription has expired or your account is inactive, proxies will no longer connect.
3. **Incorrect Proxy Format** – Double-check that you are using the correct proxy string (IP:Port or User:Pass@IP:Port), depending on your setup.
4. **Target Website Blocks** – Some websites actively block certain proxies. In this case, switching to another proxy pool, location, or session type may resolve the issue.
5. **Network or Firewall Restrictions** – Local firewalls, VPNs, or network restrictions may interfere with proxy connections. Testing on a different network can help identify this.

If you’ve checked the above and still face issues, please contact our support team with the error details and test logs. We’ll be happy to investigate and assist further.
