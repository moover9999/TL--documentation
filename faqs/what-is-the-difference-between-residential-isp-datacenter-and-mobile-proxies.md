# What is the difference between residential, isp, datacenter and mobile proxies?

* **Residential:** IPs assigned by ISPs; ideal for scraping and automation; low block rates.
* **ISP (Static Residential)**: Real residential IPs purchased directly from ISPs but hosted in data centers. They combine datacenter speed with residential trust, making them stable, fast, and harder to detect.
* **Datacenter:** Fast, affordable, but more likely to be blocked.
* **Mobile:** IPs from mobile carriers; excellent for social media or app testing; very low block rates.
