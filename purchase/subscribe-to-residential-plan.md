# Subscribe to Residential plan

{% hint style="info" %}
This section provides information on how to subscribe to a recurring product. You have the option to select the desired provider and purchase the required data accordingly.
{% endhint %}

1. First you have to navigate to **Purchase -> Residential Proxies -> Subscription**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 9 .11.42.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. You can see the available **plans** with their features and the cost per month.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 9 .15.22.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Select the **quantity** to be subscribed based on the required product.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 9 .29.06.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Then click on the **Subscribe** button&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 9 .30.02.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. To check the order history, navigate to **Billing and History -> Subscription.** You can see your subscription purchase history under "**Subscription History**"

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 9 .37.13.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
