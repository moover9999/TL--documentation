# Credit Allocation

1. Navigate to **Purchase -> Residential Proxies -> Billing & History**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/1.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can allocate the credits to each product using this.

2. Click on **the Allocate** Button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. A confirmation pop-up window will appear and you can click 'Yes' to proceed.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/3.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. You can see the Credit Allocation History on the same page and it can be filtered by multiple periods.
