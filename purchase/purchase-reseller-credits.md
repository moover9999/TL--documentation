# Purchase Reseller Credits

{% hint style="info" %}
This guide explains how to purchase credits for allocating data among providers and sub-users.
{% endhint %}

1. To purchase Credits, you have to navigate through **Purchases -> Residential Proxies -> Purchase**.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 7 .38.03.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Select "**Pay as you go**" section

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 7 .41.40.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Select the **number of credits** required to be purchased using the **slider** on account credits. You can review the data balance after purchase in the top right corner of the section.

{% hint style="info" %}
1 Credit = 1$
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 7 .46.12.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Then click on the **Purchase** button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 7 .47.27.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
