# Manage Subscription

{% hint style="info" %}
This guide will help you set up subscription management within Stripe and will help you customize the functionality of the customer portal.
{% endhint %}

1. Based on your subscription product, you can navigate to the below location

* Residential Proxy Subscriptions: **Purchase -> Residential Proxies -> Billing & History -> Subscriptions**
* ISP Proxy Subscriptions: **Purchase -> ISP Proxies -> Billing & History**

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-07 at 9 .00.33.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Based on the section you are in, you can see the all subscriptions there

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-07 at 9 .02.58.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Click on the **Manage Subscriptions** button

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-07 at 9 .01.40.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Here you can select the plans and cancel as you want.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2024-02-28 14_19_40-Proxies Billing.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
