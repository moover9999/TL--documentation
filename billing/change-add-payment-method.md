# Change/Add Payment Method

{% hint style="info" %}
This guide will help you update or change your payment method through the Stripe customer portal. You can securely add a new card or replace your existing payment method at any time.
{% endhint %}

**Step 1: Navigate to Your Subscription Section**

Depending on your active subscription product, go to the appropriate section in your dashboard:

* **Residential Proxy Subscriptions:**\
  `Purchase → Residential Proxies → Billing & History → Subscriptions`
* **ISP Proxy Subscriptions:**\
  `Purchase → ISP Proxies → Billing & History`

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-07 at 9 .00.33.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Once you’re in the relevant section, you will be able to view all your active subscriptions.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-07 at 9 .02.58.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2: Open Subscription Management**

Click on the **Manage Subscriptions** button.\
This will redirect you to the secure Stripe customer portal where you can manage your billing details and payment methods.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-07 at 9 .01.40.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3: Update or Add a Payment Method**

Inside the Stripe customer portal:

1. Select **Payment Methods** or **Billing Information**.\
   ![](<../.gitbook/assets/1 (6).png>)
2.  Click **Add payment method** to add a new card or payment option.<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2 (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3.  To change your existing method, add the new payment method first and set it as the default.<br>

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/3 (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4. You may remove old payment methods once the new one is successfully added.<br>

**Step 4: Confirm Changes**

After adding or updating your payment method, ensure the correct card is set as **default** for future subscription charges.\
All upcoming renewals will automatically use the updated payment method.

If you experience any issues while updating your payment details, please contact our support team and we’ll be happy to assist.
