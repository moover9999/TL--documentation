# Create Sub-users

1. First, navigate to **Residential Proxies -> Users.**&#x20;

{% hint style="info" %}
All the sub-users are displayed in this section along with their user ID, plan name, and created date.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 10 .39.31.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Next, click on the **Create New User** button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 10 .44.37.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. Select the **provider** from the drop-down list.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 10 .45.17.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4. Then **add/remove** data. Positive values will add data and negative values will remove the data.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 10 .45.53.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

5. Finally, click the **Create** button.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/CleanShot 2024-12-06 at 10 .48.48.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
