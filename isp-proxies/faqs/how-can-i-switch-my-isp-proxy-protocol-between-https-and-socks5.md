# How Can I Switch My ISP Proxy Protocol Between HTTPS and SOCKS5?

## Switching Proxy Protocols

TorchProxies ISP plans support multiple proxy protocols. This guide explains how to switch between them using the Torch proxies dashboard.

***

### Supported Protocols

| Protocol     | Port  |
| ------------ | ----- |
| HTTP / HTTPS | 61234 |
| SOCKS5       | 62234 |
| UDP          | 65535 |

> **Important:** Protocol switching is only available for one port at a time

***

### Switch via My Products

#### Step 1 - Log in to the Dashboard

Access the [TorchProxies Dashboard](https://dashboard.torchproxies.com/) and sign in with your credentials.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/0 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### Step 2 - Navigate to My Products → ISP Proxies

From the top left navigation, click **My Products**, then select **ISP Proxies**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/1 (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### Step 3 - View Your Orders

Once **ISP Proxies** is selected, you will see the management page for your ISP proxy orders.

* Go to **My Proxies**
* Set the filters to **All Time** to display all your orders.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/3 (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### Step 4 - Select the Proxy Order

Locate the **Order ID** or **Plan Name** for the proxy package where you want to switch the protocol.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/4 (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### Step 5 - Access The Proxy List

You will see several available actions, such as:

* **Copy**
* **Download**
* **View**

Click **View** to proceed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/5 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### Step 6 - Choose the Protocol

Once the proxy list loads after clicking **View**, scroll below the proxies and you will see protocol options:

* **HTTP/HTTPs**
* **SOCKS5**
* **UDP**

Select the protocol you want to use.

<div><figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/6 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/7 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

***

#### Step 7 - Copy and Use the Proxies

After selecting the protocol:

* The **port numbers will automatically change** in the proxy list.
* Copy the updated proxy details.
* Use them according to your required use case.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/8 (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

✅ **Tip:** Always ensure you select the correct protocol required by your software or application before copying the proxy details.

***

### Summary

|                         | Details                               |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Access via**          | My Products → ISP Proxies             |
| **Supported protocols** | HTTP/HTTPS, SOCKS5 and UDP            |
| **Port updates**        | Automatically upon protocol selection |
