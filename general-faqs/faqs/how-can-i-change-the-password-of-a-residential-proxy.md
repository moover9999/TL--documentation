# How can I change the password of a residential proxy?

At this time, changing the password for residential proxies is not supported. However, we provide **IP whitelist authentication** as an alternative. This method allows you to securely access your proxies without a username or password. You can easily manage your authorized IPs directly from your dashboard, giving you instant access and control over who can use your proxies.\
