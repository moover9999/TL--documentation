# What's the difference between the Basic and Premium Residential plans?

Our Residential plans are designed to meet different needs:

* **Basic Residential Plan:** Provides access to our standard IP pool, suitable for general browsing, light scraping or everyday tasks. It offers reliable connectivity at a cost-effective rate.
* **Premium Residential Plan:** Offers a specialized pool of optimized IPs designed for more demanding use cases. These IPs provide higher stability, lower block rates and superior performance. Ideal for high-volume scraping, ticketing or scenarios where reliability and speed are critical.

In short, **Basic** is perfect for general use, while **Premium** is tailored for advanced tasks that require consistent and robust performance.
