# Remove your Whitelisted IPs

**Overview**\
In this guide, we will walk you through the process of removing whitelisted IP addresses from your proxy product. Removing an IP ensures that the specified address no longer has access to the proxy, helping you maintain security and control over authorized usage.

## How to Remove Whitelisted IPs

**Step 1: Navigate to Residential Proxies**

* Go to **My Products** and then select **Residential Proxies**.
* Next, click on **Users**.

{% hint style="info" %}
Always make sure to select on "**All time**" for the period.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 2: Select a Relevant User**

* Choose the user for whom you want to remove the whitelisted IPs.
* Click on the **View** button to access their settings.

{% hint style="info" %}
This page also displays the user associated with the whitelisted IP, as shown in the screenshot below.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/1 (4).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 3: Locate the Whitelisted IPs Section**

* Scroll down to the **Whitelisted IPs** area.
* Here you will see a list of IP addresses that are currently whitelisted.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/2 (3).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 4: Remove the IP Address**

* Find the IP address you want to remove and click on the **Remove** (or Delete) option next to it. \[As shown in the image above, indicated by the circle.]
* Confirm the action if prompted. \[As shown in the image below.]

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/3 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Step 5: Save Changes**

* After removing the required IPs, click **Add IPs** to ensure your whitelist is updated.
* The removed IPs will no longer have access to your proxy service.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/4 (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### Additional Notes:

* If you accidentally remove an IP, you can re-add it at any time by following the whitelisting steps.
* Some plans may restrict the number of IP modifications you can make within a certain period.
* Always double-check that you are editing the correct user and proxy product before saving changes.
