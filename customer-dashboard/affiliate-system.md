# 🫐 Affiliate System

{% hint style="info" %}
#### Affiliate Programme Overview

The system includes an **affiliate programme** that allows customers to earn commissions by referring new users.

Each eligible customer is assigned a **unique affiliate link** from the customer dashboard. This link can be shared with others to invite users to join the platform. When a user registers using an affiliate link, they are permanently associated with the corresponding affiliate user.

Whenever a referred user makes a **purchase**, the purchase is attributed to the affiliate. For each successful purchase, a **commission** is allocated to the affiliate based on the commission rate defined for the Associate Plan.

The affiliate programme currently supports **both residential and ISP plan purchases**, with the ability to extend support to additional plan types in the future.

Details of the credentials of the supported providers can be inserted by administrators from the **Admin Dashboard → More Settings** section.

#### Introduction to the Affiliate System

Welcome to our **Affiliate System**, a program designed to reward your users for promoting our services! By participating, your users can earn **commissions** simply by referring new customers to our platform.

#### How It Works:

1. **Get Your Affiliate Link:** Each eligible customer will get a **unique affiliate link** from their dashboard.
2. **Share the Link:** Share the link with friends, family, or anyone interested in our services.
3. **Earn Commissions:** When a referred user registers using your link and makes a **purchase** (residential or ISP plan), a commission is allocated to the affiliate based on the Platforms commission rate.
4. **Provider Credentials:** Details of the supported providers can be configured by administrators from the **Admin Dashboard → More Settings** section.

Our affiliate system is a **win-win**, allowing your users to earn rewards while helping grow the platform community.
{% endhint %}
