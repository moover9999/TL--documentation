# 💰 Affiliate System

{% hint style="info" %}
#### Affiliate Programme Overview

The system includes an **Affiliate Programme** that allows customers to earn commissions by referring new users.

Each eligible customer is assigned a **Unique Affiliate Link** from the customer dashboard. This link can be shared with others to invite users to join the platform. When a user registers using an affiliate link, they are permanently associated with the corresponding affiliate user.

Whenever a referred user makes a **purchase**, the purchase is attributed to the affiliate. For each successful purchase, a **commission** is allocated to the affiliate based on the commission rate defined for the Associate Plan.

The affiliate programme currently supports **both Residential and ISP plan purchases**, with the ability to extend support to additional plan types in the future.

Details of the credentials of the supported providers can be inserted by administrators from the **Admin Dashboard → More Settings** section.

#### Introduction to the Affiliate System

Welcome to our **Affiliate System**, a program designed to reward your users for promoting our services! By participating, your users can **earn commissions** simply by referring new customers to our platform.

#### How It Works:

1. **Get Your Affiliate Link:** Each eligible customer will get a **Unique Affiliate Link** from their dashboard.
2. **Share the Link:** Share the link with friends, family, or anyone interested in our services.
3. **Earn Commissions:** When a referred user registers using your link and makes a **purchase** (residential or ISP plan), a commission is allocated to the affiliate based on the Platforms commission rate.
4. **Provider Credentials:** Details of the supported providers can be configured by administrators from the **Admin Dashboard → More Settings** section.

Our affiliate system is a **win-win**, allowing your users to earn rewards while helping grow the platform community.
{% endhint %}

### Setting Up Affiliate Campaign on [Referly](https://www.referly.so/) (formally known as Pushlap Growth)

Once logged in, follow the steps below to set up your affiliate campaign in the [**Referly**](https://www.referly.so/) Dashboard.



1. From the dashboard, click **“Create New Program”** to start setting up your affiliate campaign.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled design (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

2. Enter the basic campaign details, including the **Campaign Program Title** and your preferred **Subdomain**.\
   \
   Make sure the title clearly represents your program, and choose a subdomain that aligns with your brand or platform name.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled design (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

> #### Make sure to select “**aff**” as the Affiliate URL Tracking Method.

Choosing the correct tracking method ensures referrals are properly recorded and commissions are accurately attributed.

{% hint style="info" %}
You must select **“aff”** from the available keyword options.

Choosing any other keyword may prevent the Affiliate Programme from functioning correctly on your dashboard, as referral tracking depends on this exact setting.
{% endhint %}

3.  Enter your **Dashboard URL** in the **Landing Page URL** field.

    \
    This ensures that users who click an affiliate link are correctly redirected and tracked within your platform.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled design (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

4.  Customize the **commission rate** and configure the remaining program details directly from the dashboard.<br>

    Adjust the settings according to your business model to ensure commissions are calculated and applied correctly.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/FireShot Capture 186 -  - [www.referly.so].png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### **Important**

Once the Affiliate Programme is configured, generate your **API Key** from:

**Settings → Advanced** in the Referly Dashboard.\
\
The API key is required to properly connect and synchronize the affiliate system with your platform.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Untitled design (9).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

You can obtain the **Campaign Program ID** directly from the Referly Dashboard URL.

After opening your campaign, check the page address in your browser:

`referly.so/dashboard/campaign/<campaign-program-id>`

The value shown in place of `<campaign-program-id>` is your Campaign Program ID.
